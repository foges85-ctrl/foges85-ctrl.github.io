CertFlash Privacy Policy

Operator: Fogarty Holdings LLC ("we", "us")
Effective date: November 12, 2026

CertFlash is a flashcard app for IT certification exams. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: CertFlash has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your study data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The only time anything is sent to us is when you use the optional CertFlash Coach subscription and have agreed to it on that device.

What CertFlash does not do

  • No account. You never sign up, and we never ask for your name or email address.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It uses only Apple's own frameworks. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Your study data

Your study progress, favorites, study streak and exam dates are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also synced between your devices through your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage.

That data goes to Apple, not to us. We do not run a server that receives it, and we cannot see it.

Study reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent to us to deliver them.

Purchases

The CertFlash Pro unlock and the CertFlash Coach subscription are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

CertFlash Coach (optional subscription)

CertFlash Coach adds a daily study plan, plain-language explanations of a card's answer, and answers to questions you ask about a card. The explanations, answers and plan notes are written by an AI model and can be wrong. Each subscriber can make up to 300 Coach requests a month.

When anything is sent

Nothing is sent until you tap Allow on the "Send to Anthropic?" prompt. That prompt tells you what is sent, including the Daily Plan numbers described below. Your choice is stored on that device only, so agreeing on one device does not agree for another. If we widen what Coach sends, we ask again.

After you have agreed, a request is sent:

  • when you tap to have a card explained;
  • when you ask a question about a card;
  • at most once a day, when you open the Daily Plan, to fetch a short note about that day's plan.

What is sent

RequestWhat is sent
Explain a cardThe card's deck name, question and answer.
Ask about a cardThe same, plus the question you typed (up to 500 characters).
Daily plan noteFor each deck in today's plan: the deck name, the days left until your exam, and how many cards are due, weak and new. No card text and no dates.

Every request also includes Apple's signed record of your Coach subscription purchase. We use it only to check with Apple that your subscription is active and to count your requests. No name, email address, device identifier or account is sent.

Who receives it

  1. Our server, run on Cloudflare. Requests go to our server, which runs on Cloudflare Workers. It checks your subscription, counts the request against the monthly limit, and passes the request on.
  2. Apple. Our server sends your subscription's original transaction ID to Apple's App Store Server API to confirm the subscription is active.
  3. Anthropic. Our server sends the card text, your question or the plan numbers to Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI model and writes the reply. Your subscription record and transaction ID are not sent to Anthropic.

What our server stores

Our server stores two small records. Neither contains your transaction ID itself. Each is filed under a one-way keyed hash of that ID, so the record cannot be traced back to your purchase without our secret key.

RecordWhat it holdsDeleted automatically after
Subscription statusWhether the subscription was active at the last check1 hour if active; 5 minutes if not
Monthly request countThe number of Coach requests this month40 days after the month's last request

These records expire and are deleted by Cloudflare's storage on their own.

What our server never stores or logs

Our server does not store or log the card text, your questions, the AI's replies, the plan numbers, your subscription record or your transaction ID. For each request it produces one log line: the request type, a result code, and how long the request took. That line is not stored. We can see it only live, while we are actively watching the server's output.

What Cloudflare handles

To deliver requests to our server, Cloudflare processes network information such as your IP address, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We have turned off Cloudflare's stored request logs for our server, so Cloudflare keeps no per-request log for us, and we do not keep your IP address.

What Anthropic does with requests

Anthropic receives Coach requests under its commercial terms for API customers.

  • Training. Anthropic's commercial terms say: "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services." (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms)
  • Retention. Anthropic says: "For Anthropic API users, we automatically delete inputs and outputs on our backend within 30 days of receipt or generation," with exceptions. Among them: if a request is flagged by Anthropic's automated trust and safety systems as violating its Usage Policy, Anthropic may keep the inputs and outputs for up to 2 years, and it may keep data where the law requires it. (https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866)

The Report button

If an AI answer looks wrong, Report opens a draft email in your mail app to contact@fogartyholdings.com. The draft contains the card's question, the AI's answer and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Deleting your data

On your device. Deleting CertFlash from your device removes the app's data from that device, including your Coach consent and any saved plan note.

In the app. You can remove a favorite or clear an exam date in the app, and the change syncs to your other devices. CertFlash does not currently have a way to erase your study progress or streak, or to delete everything at once.

In iCloud. If you use iCloud, a copy of your study progress, favorites, streak and exam dates is kept in your iCloud account. The app never deletes that copy. Deleting the app does not delete it: if you install CertFlash again while signed in to the same iCloud account, the app restores your data from it. Turning off iCloud for CertFlash stops that device from syncing, and CertFlash keeps working with the data on the device, but it does not delete the copy already in iCloud.

On our server. We hold nothing about your study data. The Coach records described above delete themselves on the schedule shown.

For help deleting your data, contact contact@fogartyholdings.com.

Withdrawing consent

Go to the Progress tab and turn off Send to Anthropic. From then on, nothing is sent from that device. The next Coach request asks for your permission again.

Canceling the Coach subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. In CertFlash, open the Progress tab and tap the CertFlash Coach row to open your subscription settings. You can also open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap CertFlash Coach, and tap Cancel Subscription (https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428). Canceling stops future charges. Our server's records about your subscription expire on the schedule above.

Children

CertFlash is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask about or delete personal information held about you. We hold no name, email address or account information for CertFlash users; the only records on our server are the short-lived Coach records described above. To ask a question or make a request, contact contact@fogartyholdings.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If a change widens what CertFlash Coach sends, the app will ask for your permission again before sending anything.

Contact

Fogarty Holdings LLC: contact@fogartyholdings.com
