Security+ SY0-701 Acronym List

Every acronym in CompTIA’s official SY0-701 acronym list, with a plain-English explanation of each one.

Sean Fogarty CompTIA A+ Certified · Network+ in progress
Updated October 2026
⚡ Quick Answer
CompTIA’s official Security+ SY0-701 acronym list has 336 entries covering 330 acronyms. MAC has three meanings, and PAM, RA, RBAC and SAN each have two. All 336 entries are in the table below.

This is the acronym list from the official CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 exam objectives (document version 7.0). CompTIA describes it as the acronyms that appear on the SY0-701 exam. The “Stands for” column gives each expansion exactly as CompTIA writes it. Where CompTIA’s wording differs from the name most sources use, the “What it is” column often notes the usual name. Exam details are on CompTIA’s Security+ certification page.

This list is for SY0-701. SY0-801 (V8) launches on or around November 17, 2026 and has its own acronym list; SY0-701 remains available until June 11, 2027 (English).
